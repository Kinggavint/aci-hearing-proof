Driving Directions from Youngsville to ACI Hearing Center

ACI Hearing Center is about 9 miles and roughly 12 minutes from downtown Youngsville, depending on time of day. Most Youngsville patients come one of two ways.

  1. From downtown Youngsville, head north on Ambassador Caffery Parkway toward Lafayette.
  2. Continue past the LA-733 (Chemin Metairie) intersection.
  3. Turn right onto Kaliste Saloom Road.
  4. Turn left onto Saint Thomas Street. ACI Hearing Center is at 103 Saint Thomas Street on the right.

Typical drive time: 10 to 13 minutes.

Route 2 — LA-89 (Verot School Road) scenic backup

  1. From Youngsville, take LA-89 (Verot School Road) north toward Lafayette.

  3. Turn left onto Kaliste Saloom Road.
  4. Turn right onto Saint Thomas Street. Look for our building on the left.

Typical drive time: 13 to 16 minutes.

Parking: Free on-site parking directly in front of the building. Wheelchair-accessible entrance on the ground floor. Handicap parking spaces adjacent to the front door.

Services Available to Youngsville Patients

  • Custom Ear Molds. Swim plugs, musician plugs, sleep plugs, hunter and shooter protection.
