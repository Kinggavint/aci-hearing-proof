What a hearing aid treatment plan at ACI costs

Treatment plans at ACI Hearing Center range from $4,500 to $7,500. Each plan includes:

  • Binaural hearing aids (both ears)
  • Real-ear measurement fitting so the aids match your specific ear canal
  • Follow-up adjustments as your hearing needs shift
  • Ongoing cleanings and maintenance
  • Our care commitment for the life of your devices

The price range covers different technology tiers, feature sets, and manufacturer choices. Your Doctor of Audiology will match a plan to your hearing loss, your lifestyle, and your budget. We put the full number in writing before you commit. No surprise fees.

Lyric extended-wear is a subscription and is billed separately from the standard treatment plan. See our Lyric hearing aids page for subscription details.

Two financing partners, one goal: a monthly payment that works for you

CareCredit

CareCredit is the largest healthcare financing network in the US, with millions of cardholders. If you already have a CareCredit card for dental, vision, or veterinary work, you can use it for hearing aids at ACI. Promotional financing is available with 0 percent APR terms of 6, 12, 18, or 24 months on qualifying purchases. Longer 36 to 60 month plans at reduced APR are also offered.

PowerPay

PowerPay is often the best option for patients who prefer longer terms with predictable fixed monthly payments. Terms up to 60 months are common. Rates depend on your credit profile, and pre-qualification uses a soft credit check that does not affect your score. Good option if you want the lowest monthly payment.

Which one is right for you? CareCredit are the go-to for 0 percent APR promotional windows. PowerPay is the go-to for longest term and lowest monthly payment. Your ACI provider can pre-qualify you with all three during your appointment, and you pick the offer that fits best.

Insurance, Medicare, and Medicaid coverage

Before you finance a dollar, we verify what your insurance will pay. Sometimes it is more than you expect.

  • Louisiana Medicaid. Covers hearing aids for beneficiaries under 21 through the EPSDT benefit. Adult Medicaid coverage is limited. ACI verifies eligibility before your appointment.
  • Medicare Advantage. Many MA plans include a hearing aid benefit ranging from $500 to full coverage of specific device tiers. Coverage varies by plan year and carrier. Original Medicare (Parts A and B) does NOT cover hearing aids.
  • Employer and union plans. Blue Cross Blue Shield of Louisiana, Humana, United Healthcare, and several union plans include partial hearing aid coverage. Sometimes it is a flat allowance ($1,000 to $3,000 per ear every 3 to 5 years). Sometimes it is a percentage after deductible.
  • Louisiana Rehabilitation Services (LRS). If your hearing loss affects your ability to work, LRS may cover the hearing aids and evaluation. Applies to working-age adults.
  • Veterans Affairs. Veterans enrolled in VA healthcare typically receive hearing aids at no cost through the VA audiology clinic. ACI does not currently participate as a VA Community Care provider but can assist with documentation for VA appointments.

ACI verifies your specific plan and gives you the out-of-pocket cost in writing before you decide.

Using FSA or HSA funds for hearing aids

Hearing aids are listed as a qualified medical expense in IRS Publication 502. That means you can use pre-tax money from:

  • Flexible Spending Account (FSA). Employer-sponsored, use-it-or-lose-it by year end. If you have FSA funds sitting unspent, hearing aids are one of the biggest single items you can spend them on before they expire.
  • Health Savings Account (HSA). If you have a high-deductible health plan, HSA funds roll over year to year and can pay for hearing aids at any time. Contributions are tax-deductible.

Eligible expenses at ACI include the hearing aids themselves, the diagnostic hearing evaluation, the real-ear measurement fitting, follow-up adjustments, batteries, and consumable supplies.

Tax tip. Save the itemized receipt from ACI. If you paid out of pocket rather than through FSA or HSA, hearing aid expenses may still be deductible as unreimbursed medical expenses if they exceed 7.5 percent of your adjusted gross income. Consult your tax professional.

The 60-day trial period

ACI Hearing Center offers a 60-day trial on standard treatment plans. Take the hearing aids home. Wear them at church, at the restaurant, at the grandkids' recital. If they do not work for you within 60 days, you can return them for a refund minus any restocking fee disclosed in your written agreement.

If you financed through CareCredit, or PowerPay and return the hearing aids, ACI works with the lender to cancel or adjust the loan. You are not stuck with monthly payments on devices you returned.

Lyric subscription hearing aids are billed separately and are non-refundable once fitted (the device is designed for extended in-canal wear). See the Lyric page for details.

How to apply for financing

  1. Book your diagnostic hearing evaluation. Call 337-223-9448 or book online. The evaluation confirms your hearing loss and the treatment plan that fits.
  2. Get your written treatment plan quote. Your Doctor of Audiology gives you a full breakdown of the plan, what is included, and the total cost.
  3. Pre-qualify with CareCredit, or PowerPay. Quick online applications with soft credit checks. You can do this from your phone in the office. We help you compare offers.
  4. Pick the offer that fits. Shortest term with 0 percent APR, or longest term with lowest monthly payment. Your call.
  5. Walk out with your hearing aids fitted the same day, or scheduled for fitting. Loan payments start according to the lender's schedule.
