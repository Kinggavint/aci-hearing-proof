Audiology Care for Scott Residents

Driving Directions from Scott to ACI Hearing Center

ACI Hearing Center is about 6 miles from downtown Scott, and most Scott patients arrive in 6 to 10 minutes depending on traffic. There are two common routes.

Route 1: I-10 East (fastest at rush hour)

  1. From downtown Scott, get on I-10 eastbound.
  2. Take Exit 100 for Ambassador Caffery Parkway.
  3. Turn south (right) onto Ambassador Caffery Parkway.
  4. Turn left onto Kaliste Saloom Road, then left onto Saint Thomas Street. ACI Hearing Center is at 103 Saint Thomas Street on the right.

Typical drive time: 6 to 8 minutes.

Route 2: Cameron Street (surface streets, no interstate)

  1. From downtown Scott, take Cameron Street east.
  2. Cameron becomes University Avenue as you enter Lafayette. Continue east.
  3. Turn south (right) onto Ambassador Caffery Parkway.
  4. Turn left onto Kaliste Saloom Road, then left onto Saint Thomas Street.

Typical drive time: 9 to 12 minutes.

Parking: Free on-site parking directly in front of the building. Wheelchair-accessible entrance on the ground floor. Handicap parking spaces adjacent to the front door.

Ride share and public transit: Uber and Lyft both serve the route from Scott. The Lafayette Transit System does not currently service Saint Thomas Street directly. Older Scott patients frequently arrange a ride share or family drop-off.

Services Available to Scott Patients

Every audiology service Scott patients need is available at our Lafayette office. No long-distance trips required. Scott patients who previously drove to Houston or New Orleans for cochlear implant mapping, Lenire-certified tinnitus care, or pediatric audiology can now have it done locally.

  • Comprehensive Hearing Tests. Full diagnostic audiogram, speech-in-noise testing, tympanometry, otoacoustic emissions.
  • Hearing Aid Fitting. Oticon, Phonak, ReSound, Starkey, Widex, Signia, Unitron, Lyric.
  • Lyric Extended-Wear Hearing Aids. 24/7 wear, placed deep in the ear canal by a certified audiologist. Only certified Lyric provider close to Scott.
  • Tinnitus Management. Certified Lenire provider, sound therapy, TRT, CBT referral.
  • Cochlear Implant Evaluation and Mapping. Candidacy testing, surgical referral, lifetime mapping.
  • Diagnostic Testing. Comprehensive audiologic diagnostic testing including ABR and ECochG.
  • Custom Ear Molds. Swim plugs, musician plugs, sleep plugs, hunter and shooter protection.
  • Hearing Protection. Custom and over-the-counter solutions for work, music, and recreation.
  • Hearing Aid Repair. In-house repair on most major brands regardless of where the device was purchased.

  • Noise-induced hearing loss (industrial, hunting, music)
  • Conductive and mixed hearing loss
  • Tinnitus (ringing, buzzing, hissing)
  • Cerumen (earwax) blockage
  • Sudden sensorineural hearing loss
  • Cochlear implant candidacy
  • Pediatric hearing concerns
  • Single-sided deafness
  • Occupational hearing hazards
  • Auditory processing concerns
  • Hyperacusis and noise sensitivity
